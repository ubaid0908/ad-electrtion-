Our Story

15 years of powering dreams.

AD Electrician began in 2010 as a small two-person team with one promise — deliver electrical work that's safe, reliable, and beautifully done. Fifteen years later, we've grown into a trusted name with over 50 certified electricians serving thousands of clients across the city.

Our mission is simple: combine traditional craftsmanship with modern technology to deliver electrical solutions that last a lifetime. From a single switch to a complete smart-home setup, every project gets the same care and precision.

We're licensed, insured, and proud members of the National Electrical Contractors Association. Every team member undergoes 100+ hours of training annually to stay ahead of safety codes and tech trends.
